PRESIDENTIAL ELECTION SIMULATOR — Beta v1.4.1
=============================================

A political-campaign adventure + card-debate game: gather opinions in the
neighborhood, pick your party, answer debate questions with your cards, and
win five rounds per chapter — all the way to the presidency.

HOW TO RUN
----------
After unzipping this folder:

  * Mac:      double-click "Start Game.command" — the game opens in your browser.
  * Windows:  open a terminal in this folder and run:
                  python -m http.server 8800
              then open  http://localhost:8800/  in your browser.
              (Requires Python installed.)

  ! Do NOT double-click index.html directly — browser security blocks the
    game data from loading. Use one of the methods above.

COLLECT ALL THE EVIDENCE in a chapter, rate the game, and you will receive
a one-time lottery code — submit it to the organizer to enter the prize draw.

You can also play online (no download needed):
  https://ifromyx1980.github.io/campaign-game/

TIPS
----
  * Best played in a desktop browser (Chrome / Edge / Safari).
  * On a phone, rotate to landscape.
  * INVESTIGATION: use the arrows to move between locations, click characters
    to talk and collect evidence cards. Progress is shown at the top.
  * DEBATE: listen to the crowd's hint in the banner, then read each card's
    text to judge what kind of answer it is — cards are deliberately unlabeled.
    Answers that resonate with your party earn a bonus (revealed after you play).
    Every card can be used every round.
  * Lost a debate? Hit Retry.
  * Bottom-left buttons: "art" switches between anime and photo art styles;
    "opening" switches between three different intros.

FEEDBACK
